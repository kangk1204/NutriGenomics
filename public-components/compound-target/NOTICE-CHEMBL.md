ChEMBL-derived data attribution
===============================

ChEMBL 37 (release 2026-05-01), EMBL-EBI, was accessed through the official
web service on 2026-10-03: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/.
The ChEMBL database is provided under CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/.
Official license information:
https://chembl.gitbook.io/chembl-interface-documentation/frequently-asked-questions/general-questions.

The small derived data and source ledgers in reports/chembl_food_20261003_v2
and reports/chembl_kd_resolved_20261003_v1 are ChEMBL adaptations and retain
CC BY-SA 3.0. Changes: selection of 12 food compounds by full Standard
InChIKey, separation of native endpoints, sequence/assay quality control,
and comparison with a preserved BindingDB food subset. Raw API responses
and molecular sequences remain in ignored data directories. This notice
does not change the license of independently authored software.

Cite: Zdrazil B et al. The ChEMBL Database in 2023: a drug discovery platform
spanning multiple bioactivity data types and time periods. Nucleic Acids
Research 52(D1):D1180-D1192 (2024). DOI:10.1093/nar/gkad1004.
